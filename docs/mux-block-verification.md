# Mux, square and tape packing: independent integration verification

This records the independent verification of the step that added tape packing; the counts in item 6 are that step's, before later changes. The existing mux pair contract and its S13 erratum remain in force; the new placement of the sweep supersedes only the placement sentences in S2 and S17, not their maps. This is a competing, droppable tape-packing change. Credit arXiv:2609.09582 section 5.3.2 for the kind of packing and arXiv:2609.05625 section VI for the mux's offset representation.

1. Before the B1 sweep, B1 at i implies subtract at i+1 when that cell exists. In reverse tape order each no-subtract run starts with its sole possible B1. Rotating the run puts B1 at the other end, so on the swept tape B1 at i implies subtract at i-1. Reversing each block's cell order therefore gives precisely the previously verified encoder's domain. The encoder's PRIMS, FREE and TICKS are identical to the independently checked 443,576-word table; no new searched table is introduced.
2. Each offset-sweep swap changes b and E only when its cell has g0=1. Each B1 step changes only the two b wires when both g0 values are zero. They leave g0 unchanged and have disjoint active conditions on any shared b; thus they commute even on arbitrary tape/E values. The implemented interleaving preserves the order among B1 steps. Independent exhaustive actual-gate checks cover all 43,680 tape/E assignments for lengths 2..7, forward and inverse from clean expected outputs.
3. At rest after Euclid the tape is the swept tape, encoded with reversed cell order within each complete block. Inverse Euclid undoes both this encoding and the interleaved sweeps. An independent integer Euclid/run-rotation oracle checks the decoded rest tape and all other wires, with reverse runs started from the expected clean state, at merged and fallback primes. An unswept-expectation control fails.
4. During a mux tick, the only permitted tape deviation at its boundary is the next cell's g0 after a B1. The following tick restores it. Consequently a block decodes one tick before its first tick and recodes one tick after its last. Independent probes of the actual ticks check every tape wire at every boundary. Structural instrumentation covers tick counts 1..45 and 383..385, verifies that every previous/current/next cell access sees a decoded block, and rejects recoding when the next tick still needs that block. At most two blocks are simultaneously decoded.
5. Independent complete-M checks compare arithmetic multiplication and clean scratch in both directions, including fallback and merged paths, two- and three-block boundaries, exact full-block termination, zero and one streamed block at q=1048573, and two streamed blocks on a fallback prime. The secp population includes the earlier independently constructed 382-tick inputs. All 29 encoder CCX removals fail over every x at q=65521. These finite checks are not all-input secp equivalence.
6. Measured whole count is 1275 wires, 3299816 CCX, 23098712 T, 12104304 flat ops, Q*T=29450857800. There are 416 encoder/inverse calls, adding 12064 CCX. Allocator peaks: Euclid both ways, B1 step and mux tick 1275; codec calls 1274; square mulsub 1170. Probing leaves emitted bytes identical. No stage peaks are added together.

Verification ran against a clean isolated checkout of that step.

The top-level domain stays on-curve or infinity, canonical encoding, zero scratch. The mux's production-budget secp BDD refusal and absence of upstream checker admission remain. Flat operations exceed the flat-upload cap, I7.charge remains pending, and no qualification is claimed.
